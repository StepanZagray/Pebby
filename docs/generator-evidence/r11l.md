## Generator and proof design

- `DIFFICULTIES == (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6)` with context indices 0–5.
- `generate(..., split=...)` produces one level. `generate_game` derives stable
  BLAKE2 child seeds and defaults to exactly six increasing tiers.
- `build_game` fails cleanly on malformed/nonmapping input, rejects
  empty/reduced/extra/reordered/duplicate/split-shifted or context-shifted
  sequences, and itself runs all six stored witnesses from reset through native
  automatic transitions. It returns levels only after the final native `WIN`.
- The constructive teacher decomposes independent centroid groups but checks
  exact pixel masks globally. Pickup legs are natively replayed after each
  sub-plan so core mutation and pickup removal are never approximated.
- Search exhaustion is `truncated`/inconclusive. Only successful native replay
  is a positive certificate; no shortest-route or impossibility claim is made.
- The 60/59 budget, native context, all solution events, and final score delta
  are recorded. The validator returns a fail-closed error list for `None` and
  nonmappings, requires strict (non-boolean) curriculum integers, rebuilds
  structural metrics and hashes, and natively replays the stored ACTION6 route.
- Geometry identities normalize translation and all D4 transforms. Gameplay
  identities additionally retain group membership, exact native core/target/
  fragment/pickup pixel patterns, and cross-object colour equality/assignment
  relationships under a canonical palette renaming. Seed, route/proof, and
  private certificate metadata are excluded. The geometry identity chooses the
  split, so split is a partition rather than a renamed RNG stream.
- All six official normalized geometry hashes are computed independently and
  rejected. Whole games also reject duplicate generated gameplay identities.
- Attempt counts and rejection reasons are bounded and emitted in every
  accepted row; `generate.last_report` also records bounded terminal failure.

  not certified shortest.

  profile similarity is calibrated from one official reference per tier and
  does not establish population equivalence.
- Training, generated-policy experiments, official learned evaluation, and
  held-out `m0r0` access were not performed.
