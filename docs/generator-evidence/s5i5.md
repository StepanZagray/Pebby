## Generator and certificates

- `official_levels()` is authoritative: `DIFFICULTIES == (1, ..., 8)` and the
  curriculum has exactly eight rows with contexts 0–7.
- `generate` requires an explicit canonical split. `generate_game` uses stable
  BLAKE2 child seeds and produces exactly eight tiers unless an explicit smoke
  subset is requested. `build_game` rejects shortened, reordered, mixed-split,
  duplicated-identity, or invalid games.
- Accepted levels are fresh procedural rod forests, colour/control groupings,
  wall geometry, targets, and routes. Geometry identities are normalized under
  translation and all D4 transforms; gameplay identities normalize arbitrary
  colour labels. Split assignment is derived from the D4 identity.
- A loop-free constructive route becomes a certificate only after target-bearing
  native replay at its declared context. First native completion must be exactly
  the final stored action; mechanic and budget counters come from that episode.
- Route evidence has `optimality_claim=false`. Every accepted action changes a
  rod on a target pin's native attachment path. Admission also requires a
  fresh-native fixed-point deletion pass, a floor-independent live compact
  search below the shortcut bound, and pin/recursive/shared/branch ablations.
  Search cutoffs are stored as `bounded_unknown`, never as impossibility proofs.
- Gameplay identity is derived from native rod/pin attachment relations and
  canonical rod geometry. Unused `pin_rods` labels and independent rod-list
  order do not affect gameplay or route-semantic identities.
- Validation enforces exact scalar/container/version/proof types before it
  recomputes native relations, mechanic/budget traces, identities, deletion,
  ablation, compact-search, collision, and replay evidence. Malformed rows
  return diagnostics rather than raising.
- The compact teacher transition model was parity-checked on 469 randomized
  official transitions across all eight contexts. Every published positive is
  still replayed on a fresh native clone. `SearchResult.exact` means that native
  positive certificate; `optimal` is separate.
