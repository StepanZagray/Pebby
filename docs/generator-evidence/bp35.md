# BP35 full-standard generator

Status: `ready`. Root accepted all nine generated tiers after independent
structural review, current-source train/validation/test whole-game wins and
native-frame comparison across all nine official/generated tier pairs.

## Native reference inventory

The immutable source defines nine grids at
`third_party/arc3_games/bp35.py:3387-3959`. Horizontal movement, falling,
moving hazards, gems and spike loss are implemented at lines 4052-4227. Click
effects are implemented at lines 4289-4399: destructibles disappear, growers
spread to four empty neighbors, bridges toggle solid/open, and gravity switches
reverse gravity. ACTION7 restores custom world history at lines 4514-4551 while
still consuming an outer action. Native caps are 64 actions for levels 1-6 and
128 for levels 7-9 at lines 4405-4465. Public actions are LEFT=3, RIGHT=4,
CLICK=6 and UNDO=7.

The generator pins those bytes as SHA-256
`e9aecb52c629c3e742276c2db04b81f91555051d8617b6cbc58bac410824867f`
and records the hash in every spec/proof.

## Generated curriculum and modes

`DIFFICULTIES == (1, ..., 9)` exactly matches the native official count and
contexts 0..8. `generate(seed, difficulty, split=...)` produces one bounded
level. `generate_game(seed, split=...)` derives stable child seeds and produces
all nine levels in increasing order. Explicit reduced sequences are labeled
`explicit_smoke_subset`. `build_game` accepts either those nine annotated specs
or nine independently generated ordered full-standard specs; it rejects reduced
curricula and inconsistent optional annotations.

The v3 grammar uses these increasing relations:

| Tier | Generated relation | Calibrated route floor |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | three destructible descents with moving-hazard timing | 10 |
| 2 | seven destructible descents, denser composition, down-spike wrong turn | 16 |
| 3 | three mixed solid/open bridge descents plus destructible and hazard | 18 |
| 4 | three-layer destructible gravity lattice, two required reversals | 18 |
| 5 | five-layer mixed destructible/bridge lattice and both spike probes | 24 |
| 6 | eight-layer bridge lattice with two required reversals | 28 |
| 7 | ten-layer denser bridge lattice with two required reversals | 34 |
| 8 | three growth shafts, supported bridge crossings and gravity exit | 45 |
| 9 | deeper growth/bridge route, destructible barrier, gravity and both spikes | 55 |

Growth tiers no longer permit the rejected walk/click shortcut. Each vertical
shaft creates growth blockers around the player. The route consumes each next
growth front while crossing a visible ledge. Initially open bridge cells must
be closed because visible spikes lie below the gaps; solid targets must reopen
to enter the next shaft. A one-row visible wall ledge blocks the camera-shifted
one-row-high bypass. The route then consumes the overhead growth cell, reverses
gravity and enters the gem row. Removing every grower makes the certified route
fail, and a bounded no-growth native search also finds no win.

Tiers 6-7 now separate the first-descent, reversed ascent and final-descent
columns with continuous visible barriers on every world row. The ascent-side
barrier has exactly a spike-backed bridge door at the bottom and a ceiling
aperture; the final-side barrier has only the ceiling aperture. A complete
visible up-spike cap prevents a middle-chamber reversal from reaching the
ceiling. The intended ascent-to-final transfer remains open at the ceiling
after the first reversal. The bottom turn is an initially open bridge
over visible down-spikes, so the player must close and cross it before the first
reversal. Opening the last first-column gate falls onto another spike. Reversing
inside the first-side compartment reaches visible up-spikes; removing the exact
trap makes that counterfactual stop losing. Switch banks are confined to the
bottom and top turn chambers, and the final column starts solid, so the second
reversal must be followed by opening every final gate. These relations close
the former six-action first-gate/final-column route structurally.

Switch-bank visibility is not part of the safety argument. The top bank can be
clicked remotely at spawn and the bottom bank can be clicked from later first-
shaft states. Independent native probes show these premature reversals lose
under the immutable cap or remain inside the continuous partition.

Every accepted spec binds its seed/attempt to the deterministic rows, profile,
construction certificate and exact spike probe plan. Validation recomputes that
grammar, raw/D4/gameplay identities and split, full action triples, native event
census, first-WIN position, frame palette and click visibility, growth ablation,
no-growth search and greedy deletion certificate. Collision ink or rejection
metadata cannot substitute for active topology. Invalid actions after WIN are
rejected. No route carries teacher proof into model input.

## Bounded correction evidence

Representative train specs for tiers 1..9 passed the strict validator and native
replay. Stored/native-greedy-reduced lengths were `13/13, 29/29, 22/22, 20/20,
31/31, 28/28, 36/36, 61/60, 63/63`. Every reduced route remained above its tier
floor and retained the required mechanics. In particular the previous tier-8
three-action `LEFT, CLICK(21,51), CLICK(33,35)` strategy does not win.

For tiers 6-7 the exact six-action `LEFT, CLICK(27,33), RIGHT x4` route does not
win. Neither do the observed tier-7 13-action initial-shelf/final-column route,
tier-6 26-action remote-ascent route, tier-6 22-action zero-closure underside
route, or tier-7 28-action shifted-underside route. Native structural probes try
transfer toward both ascent and final columns from the initial state and every
first-column arrival; all attempts are blocked without a win. The all-row
certificate compares 66 partition coordinates, the named apertures, bottom
spikes, upper trap, initial player and goal against a fresh native world. A
deliberate wall hole fails both row and native-entity checks. Representative
click and undo operations preserve wall/spike cells.

The restricted native reachability search separately exhausts every state
reachable through LEFT, RIGHT and adjacent solid-gate openings: 23 states for
tier 6 and 27 for tier 7, no cap and no win. It excludes reversal, remote
clicks, bridge closure and undo, so it remains a narrow diagnostic rather than
the structural argument.
Removing both gravity changes or all two/four bridge closures from the certified
routes does not win. A premature first-side reversal loses, while deleting its
exact up-spike makes that probe stop losing.

For tiers 8-9, removing growth made the certified route fail. Their no-growth
probe terminated with one expanded state, no truncation and no win. A tier-9
specimen's 63-action witness was a fixed point of the native deletion probe and
retained 41 growth clicks, five open-to-solid bridge changes, two
solid-to-open changes, one destructible click and one gravity reversal.

Across 64 raw seeds per tier, normalized structural relation signatures numbered
`51, 59, 24, 44, 46, 9, 9, 23, 11`. These signatures use support heights,
route/pass columns, initial gate states, growth shafts and bridge arrangements,
rather than seeds or action padding. All nine tier-6 and nine tier-7 support
topologies native-replayed, and their canonical geometry classes cover train,
validation and test. Root's fresh current-source train, validation and test
whole-game replays subsequently won all nine levels.

Focused tests cover strict validators, identity and topology mutation, illegal
and post-WIN tails, prior shortcut rejection, growth ablation, measured relation
diversity, all split partitions, independent-spec `build_game`, shared
collection and the sequential full game. See `bp35-final.md` for exact commands
and timings.

## Limits

- The contract is `ready` after root acceptance; the evidence paths above bind the independent and root checks.
- Independent structural closure passed at
  `.scratch/multigame-resume/full-standard/native-astra-bp35-chambers/review.md`;
  root's current-source train/validation/test reports and native-frame review
  also passed before the contract changed to `ready`.
- Constructive positive witnesses and the fixed-point single-action deletion
  probe do not prove shortest paths or exhaust coordinated multi-action strategy
  changes.
- Tiers 6-7 intentionally use two causally necessary gravity reversals in dense
  8/10-layer lattices, below the independently measured shipped within-level
  census of three/eleven changes.
- Generated geometry is procedural and does not copy official maps. Profiles
  match the shipped mechanics, visible shaft language and composition ranges;
  they are not statistical estimates from a one-map-per-tier reference set.
- D4 identity is a conservative duplicate guard, not a graph-isomorphism or
  topology-novelty proof.
- UNDO is verified in a separate recovery test and is not padded into winning
  routes. Spikes are certified through exact named losing counterfactuals.
- The tier-9 official witness has external demonstrated-positive provenance.
  Its pinned local replay establishes correctness, not optimality. Official
  routes remain reference-only and absent from generation/training.
- Official search still supports only pristine pinned witnesses; arbitrary
  official live-prefix recovery remains unsupported. Generated stored-route
  suffix/UNDO recovery is a separate bounded capability.
