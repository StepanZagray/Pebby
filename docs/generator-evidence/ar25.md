# AR25 full-standard audit

Status: `ready`. Root accepted the final covered-spawn correction after
independent source review and native single-/multi-shape union regressions.

## Source audit and mechanic inventory

The eight official definitions are in `third_party/arc3_games/ar25.py:718-1110`.
Native initialization, reflection rendering, completion, and controls are at
lines 1331-1384, 1555-1687, 1688-1696, and 1698-1845 respectively. History
captures all shape and mirror positions (`1309-1330`). Reflection is a
visited-coordinate breadth-first expansion. Nodes at depth 12 still enqueue
their reflections; depth-13 nodes do not expand.

Shipped mechanics:

- direct and reflected shape coverage of goal pixels;
- one or two movable shapes;
- one vertical or horizontal mirror, progressing to perpendicular horizontal
  plus vertical mirrors and recursive reflection closure;
- a fixed vertical mirror in tier 1, then movable axes;
- object selection through ACTION5 cycling and ACTION6 public display clicks;
- ACTION7 native position-history undo;
- native step budgets and the level-index-1 animation branch.

The engine also defines vertical/horizontal distance-triggered shape rotation
and horizontal-only/vertical-only reflection tags (`455`, `467`, `506`,
`1370-1374`, `1449`, `1534`, `1590`). None of those tagged shape prototypes is
instantiated by any of the eight official level definitions. Their official
incidence is therefore exactly zero. They remain explicit generator extensions
(`rotation=` and `reflection_restriction=`), with native replay tests, and are
not forced into default reference profiles.

## Native budgets

The official and generated sequence is exactly `[64, 64, 128, 128, 128, 320,
320, 320]`. Generation gates route length against per-tier calibrated ranges,
records the actual native remaining budget at first WIN, and replays in context
`difficulty - 1`. Because ACTION6 is free and a winning movement is checked
before deduction, this native remainder is intentionally distinct from the
separately recorded `action_count_budget_estimate`. `build_game()` rejects
anything except tiers 1..8 in contexts 0..7, then performs its own uninterrupted
native whole-game replay without forced transitions.

## Mechanic participation

For every accepted default spec, validation recomputes and requires:

- every movable mirror actually moves;
- every shape actually moves and contributes a goal not contributed by another
  shape at the actual first-winning native state;
- winning goals use each shipped axis orientation;
- tiers 5-8 cover at least one depth-2 recursive-reflection goal;
- alternating tiers exercise public ACTION6 display clicks or ACTION5 cycles;
- the stored action triples exactly equal the canonical constructive route;
- the route wins in the real engine and consumes no post-completion padding.

Movement participation is based on native position changes, not directional
action attempts. The evidence records both `movement_attempts` and successful
`movement_actions`; a blocked or orthogonal move cannot satisfy a shape/mirror
use gate. Native replay stops at the first completion and requires that event
to occur on the stored route's final action. Reflected/recursive goal counts,
axis participation, per-shape exclusive contribution, positions, and masks are
all derived from that first-winning native state rather than private targets.
Regression rows reproduce tier/seed 4/25, 5/77, and 7/23 as reflected/recursive
`13/0`, `17/6`, and `31/10`, with native remaining budgets `97`, `113`, and
`292` respectively.

Optional restriction witnesses use only the selected axis despite both axes
being present. Optional rotation witnesses show four or more native mask
rotations before completion. Native/symbolic initial coverage grids are compared
cell-for-cell, including restricted-reflection cases.

Generation mode is fail-closed: only `official_profile` and
`engine_extension` are accepted. Official rows receive every composition,
density, extent, action, and recursive-use gate. Extensions have a separate
explicit contract for their first-shape rotation/restriction, counts, extents,
goal bounds, and native participation. The mechanics summary is recomputed from
executable fields, and numeric versions require exact non-boolean integers.

## Native replay

All eight official tiers replay sequentially in one engine with witness lengths
`[15, 11, 40, 22, 28, 53, 37, 47]`; the final state is WIN with eight completed
levels. Each generated validator replay uses a level list long enough to place
the candidate at its intended context. The focused tests also cover a live
prefix, a real undo, replanning, JSON round-trip, display click conversion, and
whole-game replay.

## Identity and splits

`geometry_d4_sha256` is the minimum SHA-256 across all eight D4 transforms of
typed goal, shape, and mirror cell entities; colors and shape enumeration order
are normalized. `geometry_sha256` is its documented alias. `gameplay_sha256`
binds only executable public puzzle semantics: masks/colors, initial object
positions, reflection/rotation tags, fixedness/orientation, goals, native
budget, board size, and context. It deliberately excludes private target
positions/coordinates, constructive proof and mechanic evidence, and teacher
selection strategy. `canonical_spec_identity` is the separate certificate hash
that binds the public gameplay digest to those private teacher targets, route,
proof, and evidence. Split is recomputed from the D4 geometry hash (60% train,
20% validation, 20% test), never accepted as an RNG label. Validation rejects
wrong hashes, wrong partitions, official D4 copies, malformed routes, proof
changes, or evidence changes.

The generated target configuration stored in level data and the reference-only
`OFFICIAL_TARGETS` table are inputs to the constructive teacher, not facts
inferred by public-state search. They remain in private spec/teacher artifacts;
the shared collector exposes only rendered observations and public action
history to training arrays, so target metadata is not a model input.

## Bounded generation

Generation permits at most 64 internal candidates and uses a one-expansion
constructive search under the declared 10,000-work contract ceiling. Accepted
specs record all preceding rejection counters, candidate number, requested and
effective seeds, and BLAKE2 whole-game child seeds. Validators check all bounds
and seed derivations.

Default candidates additionally undergo a deterministic breadth-first search
over public mirror/shape configurations and public ACTION1-6 routes strictly
below the tier action floor. It never reads the private constructive target;
symbolic public coverage only nominates candidates, and every hit must replay
to native WIN before rejection. Native boards whose public goals are already
covered at spawn are rejected before the BFS begins, including coverage formed
only by the union of multiple shapes; this preserves the intended
unsatisfied-spawn rule and closes the native move-away/return win trigger. The
audit is capped at 25,000 dequeued configurations and may report truncation;
that work counter is neither a completeness nor an optimality claim. The
decisive tier-2 seed-6 attempt is rejected by the exact four-action public route
`LEFT, CYCLE, RIGHT, DOWN`. The eight-seed-per-tier sample (64 accepted rows)
carries recomputed bounded audits with no admitted below-floor route; no
padding or target restriction is used.

Both generation modes retain a detached JSON-ready `last_report`. Exhausted
single-tier reports preserve the terminal cause, rejection counters, accumulated
search work, and attempt/search caps. Whole-game reports additionally preserve
every child report plus the terminal child seed, ordinal, and difficulty. A
zero search limit returns `None` with an explicit `search_limit_zero` report;
it is not silently indistinguishable from construction or split exhaustion.

The pre-shortcut-audit calibration baseline generated one row for every tier in
each of the three splits (24 rows) in 2.0236 seconds. All 24 requested rows were accepted
and fully validated. Internally, 222 candidates were tried: 24 accepted, 120

All sampled rows moved every expected object, used reflection, and tiers 5-8
used recursion; 12 rows used click selection and 9 multi-object rows used cycle
selection.

Generated action ranges by tier across the three splits were: tier 1 `8-20`
(mean 13.00), tier 2 `15-22` (19.67), tier 3 `34-42` (38.00), tier 4 `25-37`
(31.67), tier 5 `14-20` (18.00), tier 6 `26-35` (31.33), tier 7 `23-38`
(28.67), and tier 8 `27-42` (32.67).

A separate uncurated probe (`seed=300501`, tier 5, validation) exhausted the
declared 64 internal candidates and returned `None`. This is a supported

and replay correctness rather than universal per-seed success.
